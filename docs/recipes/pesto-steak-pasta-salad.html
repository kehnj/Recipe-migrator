Pesto Steak Pasta Salad

A farfalle pasta salad tossed with seared NY strip steak, cherry tomatoes, marinated mozzarella, arugula, basil, and pesto.

Some quantities or steps were estimated — check the video.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz farfalle pasta
  • 1 large NY strip steak
  • steak seasoning
  • 16 oz cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 12 oz pre-marinated mozzarella balls, halved
  • fresh basil, chopped
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1/2 cup pesto
  • 2 cups arugula
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • crumbled feta cheese (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the farfalle until tender, drain, and transfer to a large bowl.
  2. Season the steak with steak seasoning and sear to your preferred doneness.
  3. Rest the steak, then cut it into bite-sized cubes.
  4. Add the cherry tomatoes, mozzarella balls, arugula, and basil to the pasta.
  5. Add the steak, pesto, and lemon juice.
  6. Toss everything together and season with salt and pepper.
  7. Top with crumbled feta if desired and serve.

Notes

Crumbled feta cheese is optional.
