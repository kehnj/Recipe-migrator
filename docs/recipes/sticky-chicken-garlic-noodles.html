Sticky Chicken & Garlic Noodles

Cook 20 min

An easy 20-minute weeknight one-skillet dinner of seasoned chicken thighs, peppers, and onions tossed with ramen or spaghetti noodles in a sticky garlic sauce.

Ingredients

Chicken and vegetables

  • 1½ lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, diced
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp chili crunch (optional)
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp oil (for cooking)
  • 2 bell peppers, diced
  • 1 onion, diced

Sticky garlic sauce

  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp minced garlic
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 3 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch

Noodles

  • 12 oz spaghetti or 4–5 packs ramen noodles, seasoning packets discarded
  • Up to 1 cup reserved noodle cooking water

Toppings

  • sweet and sour sauce
  • sesame seeds
  • green onion, sliced

Steps

  1. Toss the diced chicken with the garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, sesame oil, and chili crunch.
  2. Set a pot of water on the stove to boil.
  3. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Add the seasoned chicken and let it sear.
  5. While the chicken sears, whisk together the soy sauce, honey, hoisin sauce, minced garlic, and cornstarch until smooth.
  6. Add the bell peppers and onion to the skillet.
  7. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables soften and the chicken is cooked through.
  8. Cook the noodles according to package directions, reserving some cooking water before draining.
  9. Whisk the sauce again, pour it into the skillet, and stir until it bubbles and thickens.
  10. Add reserved noodle water a splash at a time (about ½ cup) until the sauce reaches your desired consistency.
  11. Add the drained noodles to the skillet and toss until coated in the sauce.
  12. Top with sweet and sour sauce, sesame seeds, and green onion.
  13. Serve warm.

Notes

Use reduced-sodium soy sauce if you are sensitive to salt. Chili crunch is optional for spice. For a saucier dish, use fewer noodles; spaghetti works in place of ramen. The caption's instructions also mention stirring minced garlic into the skillet for about 1 minute before adding the sauce, though the garlic is listed in the sauce. The sauce thickens quickly, so you can turn off the heat and let it sit while the noodles cook. Recipe credited to @mamasdownsouthkitchen.
